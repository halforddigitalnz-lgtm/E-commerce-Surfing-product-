Lengths and fit

Go roughly the same length as your board, or up to a foot longer.

Materials

Cord: moulded urethane, 5.5 mm (Comp) or 7 mm (Regular). Swivels: marine-grade stainless steel at both ends. Cuff: neoprene-lined with double velcro, a release tab and a hidden key pocket. Rail saver: 25 mm webbing with a velcro wrap.

Comes with a leash string fitted and a spare in the pack.

Five parts, no weak links

A leash only matters for the few seconds after a wipeout. Every part of the Tether is built for those seconds, from your ankle to your board.

  1. 1

    Release tab

    Pull the tab and the cuff opens in one move, even with cold hands or underwater.

  2. 2

    Neoprene-lined cuff

    Soft against your skin, with a hidden key pocket and double velcro that doesn't let go.

  3. 3

    Double swivels

    Stainless swivels at both ends let the cord spin freely, so it never knots around your leg.

  4. 4

    Urethane cord

    Stretches to soak up the pull when your board gets dragged, then springs back without kinking.

  5. 5

    Wide rail saver

    Spreads the load across your tail so the cord can't slice into your rail.

Made after one too many long swims

Offshore started the winter three of us snapped leashes at Manu Bay in the same fortnight.

Each one meant a long swim in and a board bouncing off the rocks. We spent the next year taking leashes apart, testing cords and swivels, and surfing prototypes until they stopped breaking.

Tether is the result: one leash, built properly, in the lengths and thicknesses people actually ride.
